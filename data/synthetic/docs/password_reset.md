# Password Reset

Employees can reset their corporate password from the identity portal.

## Steps
1. Go to https://id.company.test/reset
2. Enter your work email (`@company.test`).
3. Open the verification email and click the reset link (expires in 30 minutes).
4. Choose a new password that is at least 12 characters and not reused.
5. Sign in again on all devices.

## Notes
- Contractors must ask IT to initiate the reset.
- If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, check spam or contact `#it-help`.
- Never share temporary reset codes in chat or tickets.
